## 4. Feature detail

### Shimmer — the hero

**Say this.** *Not a reverb with a pitch trick bolted on. The pads run through
it the way they would through a shimmer on a DAW insert.*

Most shimmer plugins put the effect alongside the sound, so the pad and the
shimmer arrive as two separate things sitting next to each other. Nebula Tide
puts it in the path. The pad goes in, the shimmer comes out, and what you hear
is one sound that grows rather than two sounds stacked.

One control does the work. At the bottom of its travel the effect is a hint of
air. At the top it is a room with no ceiling. Turning it does five things at
once, each on its own curve: how much, how close, how bright, how dense, and
how far it regenerates. Underneath there is a full set of controls in Settings
for anyone who wants them, and nobody has to touch them.

The pitch shifting is splice-aligned rather than fixed-interval, which is the
difference between a shimmer that sounds smooth and one that sounds granular.

**Marketing lines.**
- "The pad rises as it fades."
- "One knob from a breath to a cathedral."
- "In the path, not beside it."

### Studio — build your own presets

**Say this.** *Twelve slots, one per key. Drop your recordings in, name it, save
it. It appears on the stage beside the ones we made.*

No folder structure to learn, no manifest to write, no restart. Drag audio onto
the key you recorded it in and the slot fills. This is the feature that turns
buyers into residents.

**Marketing lines.**
- "Your recordings, our engine."
- "Twelve slots. One per key. That is the whole interface."

### Drag to timeline

**Say this.** *Drag from the app onto a track and a MIDI clip lands, already in
the right key.*

The clip carries the pad on one channel and the FX and textures on another, so
one drag reproduces what you were hearing. This is the workflow producers know
from Stylus RMX, and nothing else in this category offers it.

**Marketing lines.**
- "From the instrument to the timeline, in one drag."

### Five platforms

Windows and macOS ship the sound library with the app. The mobile versions
download it on first launch, so the store download stays small. Same
instrument, same presets, same sound.

### Choose where the sounds live

The installer shows you the path before it writes anything and lets you change
it. Settings has the same control afterwards, so the library can move to
another drive without a reinstall. This exists because "blank screen after
install" is the single worst first impression a sound library can make.

### A protected library

The recordings ship in a single encrypted container and are never written to
disk in the clear. For the buyer this is invisible. For the studio it means the
library is a product rather than a folder of files.

### Licensing

One purchase covers two machines. Move a seat yourself, from the app or from
your account on the website. No support ticket.
