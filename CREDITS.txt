NEBULA TIDE — SOUND CREDITS
===========================

Some of the texture and FX recordings in Nebula Tide come from Freesound.
Those released under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence may be
edited and included in a commercial product, provided the original creator is
credited and the fact that the recording was changed is stated. Both are done
below.

Every recording listed here has been edited for use in Nebula Tide — trimmed,
looped, level-matched and processed — so none is the original file as
published on Freesound.
