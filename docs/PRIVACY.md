# Privacy Policy — Nebula Tide

**Amanorsac Studio**
Last updated: 11 September 2026

This policy describes what Nebula Tide does with information. It is written to
match what the software actually does, and it is short because the software
does very little.

---

## The short version

Nebula Tide has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party
tracking of any kind. It contacts one server, ours, and only for two reasons:
to activate your licence and to check whether a newer version exists.

Your presets, your recordings and your settings stay on your device. We never
receive them.

---

## What we receive, and when

### When you activate or deactivate a licence

Activating sends three things to `amanorsac.studio`:

| What | Why |
|---|---|
| Your licence key | to confirm the licence is valid and has a seat free |
| A device identifier | so the same device does not consume a second seat |
| Your device's name | so you can tell your devices apart when moving a seat |

The device identifier is a random value created the first time the app runs
and stored on that device. It is not derived from your hardware, and it tells
us nothing about your machine.

Your device's name is whatever your operating system calls it. On many
computers that includes the owner's name, for example "Stephen's MacBook". If
you would rather we did not hold that, rename your device in your system
settings before activating.

Deactivating a device sends the same identifiers so the seat can be released.

### When the app checks for updates

The app requests a small file from our server that lists the current version.
Nothing about you is included. As with any web request, our server sees the
internet address the request came from, which is unavoidable and which we do
not use to build a profile or link to your licence.

### That is the complete list

No other information leaves your device. In particular we do not receive your
audio, your presets, your MIDI, your usage, your location, your contacts, or
any identifier used for advertising.

---

## What stays on your device

- Presets you create or import, as ordinary audio files in your Documents
  folder, under Amanorsac Studio. They are yours: back them up, move them,
  delete them.
- Your licence proof, stored encrypted using the operating system's own
  facility for secrets.
- Your settings and control assignments.

Uninstalling removes the application. Your presets remain unless you delete
that folder yourself, which is deliberate: we would rather not throw away your
work.

---

## How long we keep things

Licence records are kept while the licence exists, because that is what makes
the licence work and lets you move a seat between devices. Ask us to delete
them and we will, though doing so ends the licence.

Server request logs are kept briefly for security and reliability, then
discarded.

---

## Who else sees it

Nobody. We do not sell, rent or share this information. There are no
advertising partners, no analytics providers and no data brokers involved,
because the app contains no such components.

We would disclose information if the law required it, and we would tell you
unless we were legally prevented from doing so.

---

## Children

Nebula Tide is a musical instrument for general audiences. It is not directed
at children and collects nothing beyond what is described above from anyone.

---

## Your rights

Wherever you live, you may ask what we hold about you, ask for it to be
corrected, or ask for it to be deleted. Write to the address below and we will
answer.

If you are in the United Kingdom or the European Economic Area, our basis for
processing the licence information is contractual necessity: without it the
licence cannot be issued or enforced.

---

## Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top and publish the new
version here. Material changes will be announced alongside a release.
