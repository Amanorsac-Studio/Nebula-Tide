## 2. Purpose and audience

Nebula Tide is an ambient drone pad instrument. It plays continuous, seamless
background pads recorded separately in all twelve musical keys, so a musician
can put a sustained bed of sound under a song in the right key immediately.

It is for worship musicians who need a pad in the key of the next song, ambient
and electronic producers, film and game composers, and people using ambient
sound for meditation or focus.

The problem it solves: getting a clean, correctly keyed, endlessly sustaining
pad normally means programming a synthesiser or looping an audio file that
clicks at the loop point or is in the wrong key. Nebula Tide does it in one tap.

## 3. How to access the main features

No login, account, credentials or sample files are required. All sounds are
included in the app.

1. Open the app. The main stage appears with a preset loaded.
2. Tap any planet along the key ring to play the pad in that key. Tap another
   to change key; the sound crossfades without a gap.
3. Use the arrows beside the preset name to change preset.
4. Tap the gold star for an effect sound and the red star for a texture.
5. Tap SETTINGS for the shimmer, reverb and crossfade controls.
6. Tap STUDIO to create a preset from your own audio files, one per key.
7. About is at the bottom of Settings, with version and credits.
