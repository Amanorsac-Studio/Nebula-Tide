# Nebula Tide — chord vocabulary and pad map

_Generated by `tools/chords/generate.js`. Do not edit by hand._

Every pad is a **sus2** voicing — root, major 2nd, perfect 5th, and deliberately **no third**.
That missing third is what lets one pad serve both the major and the minor chord on the same
root, plus most of their extensions. This file lists every chord in the vocabulary and the pad
that sounds under it.

## The rule in one line

> A pad on root R fits any chord on R that has a **perfect 5th** and a **natural 9th**.
> The third does not matter. Everything else needs a substitute pad.

## Substitutions

| Chord family | Pad | Interval | What the pad gives you |
|---|---|---|---|
| Everything with a perfect 5th and natural 9th | Chord root | unison | root · 9 · 5 |
| Diminished triad, m7b5, m9b5 | Minor 3rd up | +3 | b3 · 11 · b7 |
| Dominant 7b5 | Whole step up | +2 | 9 · 3 · 13 |
| Augmented, maj7#5 | Major 3rd up | +4 | 3 · #11 · maj7 |
| Altered dominants — 7b9, 7#9, 7#5, 7alt, 13b9 | Semitone up | +1 | b9 · #9 · b13 |
| Diminished 7th | **Hold previous** | — | no sus2 pad fits |
